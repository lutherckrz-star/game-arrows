How to Play:
1. Tap an arrow to launch it in its direction
2. Arrow only moves if path is clear (no other arrows blocking)
3. Clear all arrows to complete the level

Elements:
- FIRE: Destroys 1 Ice arrow in its path
- WATER: Passes through 1 blocker
- ICE: Freezes adjacent arrows for 1 second
- ELECTRIC: Zaps all Electric arrows in same row/column

Chain Reactions:
- FIRE + ICE = STEAM (clears 2 nearby cells)
- WATER + ELECTRIC = CONDUCTOR (zaps 3 nearby Electrics)
- ICE + WATER = DEEP FREEZE (freezes entire row & column)
- ELECTRIC + FIRE = EXPLOSION (destroys 1 nearby blocker)

Controls:
- Tap/Click: Select and launch arrow
- U: Undo (limited)
- H: Hint (shows movable arrow)
- ESC: Pause

Boss Battles: Every 10 levels. Watch for warning glow!

Progression:
- Earn crystals: No mistakes +1, Speed bonus +1, Daily +5
- Upgrade elements (10 crystals per level)
- Unlock modes: Endless (L50), Daily (L10), Boss Rush (L50)
