Arrow Escape — Elemental

A puzzle game where you tap arrows to clear the board. Combine elements to trigger chain reactions: Steam, Conductor, Deep Freeze, Explosion. Upgrade elements, defeat bosses, earn crystals.

Features:
- 50 campaign levels with progressive difficulty
- 4 elements (Fire, Water, Ice, Electric) with 3 upgrade tiers
- 4 chain reactions: Steam, Conductor, Deep Freeze, Explosion
- Boss battles every 10 levels
- Endless, Daily Challenge, Boss Rush modes
- Crystal economy for upgrades and rewards
- 10 achievements
- Daily rewards
- Full PWA support (offline, installable)

Target: PEGI 12
Monetization: CrazyGames SDK v3 Rewarded Video + Interstitial
Platforms: CrazyGames, Playgama
